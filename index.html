Who I Am

An engineer who builds things that fly — and think

Grounded in hardware, growing into applied AI.

I'm a B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at GLA University, Mathura, working across drone engineering, system integration, and applied AI. My hands-on journey started with FPV — building and flying multirotor and fixed-wing platforms — and has since grown into full system integration: flight controllers, ground control software, sensors, and communication modules.

As a Drone R&D Engineer at Whizz Aerospace Pvt. Ltd., I worked on UAVs and aerospace systems end to end — embedded electronics and sensors (GPS, IMU, LIDAR), flight control systems, and R&D on hybrid drone interceptors and surveillance platforms, including successful demos delivered to defence and government sectors.

Beyond airframes, I work on the systems that make drones smarter and more resilient: mesh communication, swarm technology, anti-drone systems, and non-GPS navigation. I'm now applying my growing machine learning skills to my own projects — integrating AI onto drones for autonomous navigation aimed at real-world problems.
